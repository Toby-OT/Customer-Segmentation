# Banking Customer Segmentation Dashboard

An interactive Excel analytics project designed to explore customer demographics, behavioural patterns, and segmentation opportunities within a banking customer dataset.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The project analyses 500 banking customers across the 2015–2025 period to understand customer characteristics and identify patterns that could support acquisition, segmentation, and retention decisions.

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

> **Who are the bank's key customer segments, what characteristics define them, and how can these patterns support more targeted customer acquisition?**

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

The dashboard was designed to explore:

- Which age groups represent the largest customer segments?
- How do customers differ by income and tenure?
- Which geographic and occupational groups are most represented?
- What patterns exist across customer loyalty segments?
- How have customer numbers changed over time?
- Which demographic and professional groups could be targeted for acquisition?

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The project used Microsoft Excel as the primary analytics environment.

- Interactive dashboard development
- Trend analysis
- Demographic analysis

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard explores customers across several dimensions:

### Demographics
Customer distribution across age/generational groups and other demographic characteristics.

### Income & Tenure
Analysis of customer income levels and relationships with customer tenure.

### Geography
Comparison of customer distribution across geographic locations.

### Occupation
Analysis of professional profiles within the customer base.

### Loyalty Segmentation
Exploration of customer loyalty groups and their characteristics.

### Customer Growth
Analysis of customer trends across the 2015–2025 period.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Millennials and Gen X dominate
The analysis showed that Millennials and Gen X represent the dominant customer groups, making them important segments for acquisition and engagement strategies.

### 2. Professional profiles show distinct patterns
Among the occupational groups analysed, Female Doctors and Engineers stood out as notable customer profiles.

### 3. Customer growth experienced a 2020 dip
The customer trend revealed a noticeable decline in 2020, followed by changes in subsequent years.

These patterns provide useful context for evaluating customer acquisition performance over time.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, banking teams could:

- Develop targeted acquisition campaigns around high-representation age groups.
- Explore tailored offerings for strong professional segments.
- Use demographic and occupational characteristics to improve customer targeting.
- Monitor customer growth trends to identify periods of weaker acquisition.
- Combine demographic and loyalty information when designing retention strategies.
